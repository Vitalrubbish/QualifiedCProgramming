# Format:
#   === proof_of_<VC name> ===
#   <tactic lines, WITHOUT the surrounding `Proof.` and `Qed.`>
# The tactic continues until the next `===` header or EOF.  Newlines are
# collapsed to spaces on replay, so multi-tactic proofs are written one
